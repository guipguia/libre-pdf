Privacy

Your files never leave your device. Libre PDF is a static website: opening, editing, converting and saving your PDFs all happen inside your browser. There is no server-side code, no upload, no database, no account, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.

Office to PDF downloads LibreOffice (about 80 MB) from this site the first time you use it, and your browser keeps it for next time.

Libre PDF does not store your files. When you close the tab, they are gone from the page; the results exist only where you saved them.

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the pages and protect against abuse; we do not use Cloudflare analytics or logs and receive no personal data.

The code is open source (MIT): github.com/guipguia/libre-pdf.
